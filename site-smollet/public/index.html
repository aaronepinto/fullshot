Small software, free forever

Smollet

Small, free, open source applications. Each one replaces a single-purpose product that used to be worth paying for.

Plenty of software is sold by subscription for doing exactly one thing. Capturing a page. Converting a file. Trimming a clip. That was a fair trade when the work behind it was genuinely hard to do.

It is not hard any more. So we write the replacements properly, and then we give them away.

Every Smollet application does one job, runs on your own machine, and is Apache-2.0 licensed. There is no account to create, no tier to unlock, and nothing to cancel. Each one stands on its own and stays small enough to read in an afternoon.

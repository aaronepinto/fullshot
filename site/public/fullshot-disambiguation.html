Screencappy is not "Fullshot"

Last verified: August 15, 2026

If you are looking for the Chrome extension called "Fullshot: Full Page Screenshot", this is not it. That is a separate product from a different developer, and it is unrelated to this project.

This site is Screencappy, a free and open source full page screenshot extension. The two share part of a name and nothing else.

Why do the names look similar?

This project was previously developed under the working name Fullshot and was renamed to Screencappy. The other extension is unaffiliated and was published independently.

We renamed to avoid exactly this confusion. If you arrived here looking for the other extension, its Chrome Web Store listing is the place to go.

What is Screencappy?

A free, open source Chrome extension for full page screenshots. Capture a whole page in one click, annotate it, and export as PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF.

It is licensed under Apache-2.0 with the source public, requires no account, requests no host permissions at install, and processes captures in your browser without uploading them anywhere.

Awesome Screenshot alternative: screencappy

Last verified: August 15, 2026

Awesome Screenshot is a capture, recording and sharing platform with a cloud account at its centre. screencappy is a full page screenshot tool that keeps everything on your machine.

If you want shareable links, team workspaces and screen recording, Awesome Screenshot is built for that. If you want to capture a page, annotate it, and save the file, screencappy does that for free with no account.

What does Awesome Screenshot do well?

Breadth. It covers screenshots and screen recording in one extension, with annotation, blur and instant shareable links, and it has the collaboration layer to match.

With 3,000,000 users and a 4.7 rating from 29.4K ratings on the Chrome Web Store as of today, it is one of the most widely used tools in the category. For teams that live on shared feedback links, that combination is hard to replicate.

What does Awesome Screenshot cost?

There is a free tier, and two individual paid tiers. Basic is $6 per month, or $5 per month billed annually. Professional is $8 per month, or $6 per month billed annually.

Team plans are priced per member: Workspace Premium is $10 per member per month, or $8 billed annually, with a free Workspace Lite tier capped at 5 members.

What are the free tier limits?

The free tier meters cloud storage rather than capture. You get up to 100 screenshots and 20 recordings in the cloud, resolution up to 720p, and three days of trash recovery.

Saving locally is treated separately and more generously: unlimited local screenshots, and local recordings capped at 5 minutes each. Annotation on the free tier is limited to basic tools, with the full set on the paid tiers.

Do I need an account to use Awesome Screenshot?

An account requires a name and email address, or a single sign on provider. The vendor's privacy policy explicitly offers a local save path as an alternative to cloud storage.

Whether any capture works before you sign in is not stated anywhere on the vendor's site, and several reviewers report hitting a sign in step. screencappy has no account and no sign in step at any point, so the question does not arise.

Where do my captures go?

Into cloud storage by default, which is what the paid tiers meter. The privacy policy states you have the option to save to your own computer instead.

One clause worth reading before uploading anything sensitive: the Terms of Service state that by posting content to the service you grant the company a licence to use, modify, publicly perform, publicly display, reproduce and distribute that content on and through the service. screencappy has no upload path, so no such licence is ever granted.

Which one should you use?

If your workflow is record, upload, share a link, and comment as a team, Awesome Screenshot is the right shape of tool and screencappy is not competing for that job.

If your workflow is capture a page, mark it up, and save or attach the file, screencappy does that without an account, without a storage quota, and without requesting access to every site you visit.

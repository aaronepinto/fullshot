What is Screencappy?

Screencappy is a free, open source Chrome extension that captures full page screenshots in one click, annotates them, and exports them as PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF. It is Apache-2.0 licensed, and the complete source is public on GitHub.

Everything happens on your own device. Screencappy has no account, no cloud service, no analytics and no network code at all, so captures cannot leave your machine. By default it requests no host permissions, which means it has no standing ability to read any page: access is granted by your click, for one tab, at that moment.

There is no paid tier. The annotation editor, blur and pixelate redaction, emoji stamps and PDF export are all included at no cost. Screencappy runs on Chrome, Edge, Opera and Brave, and a Firefox build is in the repository. Install it from the latest release: unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, and click Load unpacked.

Whole page, one click

Everything below the fold and off to the right, stitched pixel-perfectly. Sticky headers appear exactly once.

Private by design

No account, no cloud, no analytics, no host permissions, and zero network code. Captures never leave your device.

A real editor, free

Crop, arrows, shapes, pen, highlighter, text, emoji, and blur redaction with full undo and redo.

Export anywhere

PNG, JPEG, WebP, PDF (single page or paginated), or straight to your clipboard. Custom filename templates.

Pick any element

Hover to highlight, click to capture. Scrollable containers and iframes are captured in full, not just their visible box.

Awkward pages handled

Inner scroll containers, infinite scroll, mobile widths, and delayed captures for menus and hover states.

Turbo engine

Optional one-shot capture through the DevTools protocol: faster, seamless, and it can save a searchable text PDF.

Huge pages just work

Strip-based compositing sidesteps canvas limits; giant captures split automatically instead of failing.

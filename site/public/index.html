What is screencappy?

screencappy is a free browser extension that saves a picture of an entire web page in one click. It captures the whole page, not just the part you can see on screen. You can then draw on the picture, blur out anything private, and save it as a PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF.

Everything happens on your own computer. There is no account to make and no cloud service behind it. screencappy does not track you and has no way to send your pictures anywhere. It asks for no access to any website, so it cannot see the page you are on until you ask it to. It does use Chrome's debugging tool for a few advanced captures, and Chrome shows a banner on screen the whole time that is running.

screencappy is completely free. There is no paid version and nothing is held back: drawing, blurring, emoji stamps and saving as PDF are all included. It works with Chrome, Edge, Opera and Brave, and there is a Firefox version too. The code is open for anyone to read, under the Apache-2.0 licence.

Adding it to your browser

screencappy is on its way to the Chrome Web Store. It is not there yet, so for now you add it yourself. This is early access: it takes about a minute, and these three steps are all of it.

  1. Download and unzip

    Download screencappy.zip and unzip it. You will be left with a folder. Remember where you put it.

  2. Switch on Developer mode

    In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions. Turn on the Developer mode switch in the top right corner.

  3. Click Load unpacked

    Click the Load unpacked button and choose the folder you unzipped. That is it, screencappy is in your browser.

The same file works in Edge, Opera and Brave. On Firefox, download screencappy-firefox.zip instead. Once screencappy reaches the Chrome Web Store, installing will be one click and updates will arrive on their own.

The whole page, one click

Not just the part on your screen. Everything further down the page and off to the side, joined into one clean picture, with headers that stay put appearing only once.

Your pictures stay yours

No account, no cloud, no tracking. screencappy has no way to send anything anywhere, so what you capture stays on your computer.

Mark it up, free

Crop it, add arrows, boxes, a highlighter, text or emoji, and blur out anything private. Change your mind as often as you like: every step can be undone.

Save it your way

PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF, one long page or split into pages, or copied straight to your clipboard ready to paste. You can set how files get named.

Capture just one part

Point at what you want and click, or drag a box around it. Boxes with their own scrollbars get captured in full, not just the bit showing.

Tricky pages handled

Pages that keep loading as you scroll, pages that scroll inside a panel, and shots that need a menu held open. screencappy waits for the page to settle.

See it as a phone would

Capture a page in its phone layout without resizing your window, and save PDFs whose text you can still search. Chrome shows a banner on screen while these run.

Very long pages work

Enormous pages defeat most screenshot tools. screencappy builds these in pieces, so a huge capture splits neatly across files instead of failing.
